Power engineer, built from the grid inwards

05 / Projects

Key projects

Each one framed the way an operations team thinks: the problem, what I did, the result, and why it matters inside a data center.

MSc thesis · LUH Hannover · 2026

Experimental and Numerical Analysis of the Structural Dynamic Behaviour of a Separate Yoke of a Special Power Transformer Core under Varying Clamping Forces

Problem
Transformer core noise and vibration depend on assembly and clamping conditions, which are hard to predict before a unit is in service.
What I did
Measured vibration and acoustic emission of a separate yoke under varying clamping forces and built COMSOL FEM models coupling electromagnetic excitation to structural response.
Result
Model validated against measurements with <5% error across an analysis range up to 2,000 Hz.
DC relevance
Transformer condition monitoring, diagnostics and a predictive-maintenance mindset.
  • COMSOL
  • Vibration & acoustics
  • IEC 60076-10

BE final year project · IGNITE funded · 2022–23

Design and Development of a Phasor Measurement Unit (PMU) for Synchrophasor Data Analysis using Machine Learning

Problem
Commercial PMUs are too expensive for monitoring distribution-level grids.
What I did
As lead developer, built three low-cost PMUs (Arduino, GPS time-sync, PZEM-004T sensors) with a phasor-estimation and timestamping pipeline streaming to the cloud.
Result
40,000+ synchronised measurements; ML load prediction at 91% accuracy; selected for national IGNITE research funding.
DC relevance
Power monitoring, time-synchronised data and turning raw electrical data into decisions.
  • Arduino / C++
  • GPS sync
  • Python · scikit-learn
